CivicTrack Project Overview

Autonomous Municipal Infrastructure & Dispatch Engine

AI Department Auto-Routing

Automatically parses problem context and voice transcriptions to route complaints directly to the appropriate municipal division (e.g. Roads & Pavement, Street Lighting Bureau, Water Authority) with custom SLA targets.

Automated Duplicate Prevention

Prevents duplicate work orders by evaluating GPS proximity, municipal ward, and semantic text similarity against open tickets. Citizens can upvote existing tickets and append evidence instead of creating redundant dispatches.

Offline PWA & Background Sync

Operates smoothly in low/no connectivity environments using service worker caching and IndexedDB outbox queue. Reports sync automatically when connection is restored.
