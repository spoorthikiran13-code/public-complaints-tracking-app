1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Municipal corporations and public works departments worldwide face severe operational bottlenecks: unorganized citizen complaints, slow multi-day triage, repeated duplicate reporting of the same pothole or fallen tree, and field crews losing connectivity in dead zones.

CivicTrack solves this by introducing an end-to-end intelligent dispatch platform. Utilizing an Autonomous AI Dispatch Agent, CivicTrack instantly extracts domain vocabulary from text and voice recordings, determines the exact responsible municipal bureau (Transportation, Electrical, Sanitation, Drainage), prevents duplicate work orders through geo-fenced spatial analysis, and guarantees uninterrupted field operations through an offline-first Progressive Web Architecture (PWA).

2. Core Innovations & Engineering Modules

AI Department Auto-Routing

Parses text transcripts and audio input using semantic keyword heuristics to match issues to specific divisions (e.g., Asphalt Milling, Lighting Grid, Sewerage Bureau) with SLA metrics (8h–48h).

Duplicate Prevention Radar

Evaluates category affinity, Haversine GPS proximity (150m–350m), ward jurisdiction, and lexical similarity. Blocks redundant ticket generation and merges citizen evidence into existing active tickets.

Offline-First Service Worker

Features background IndexedDB outbox queuing and service worker caching. Reports recorded in low-reception areas are auto-synchronized and validated as soon as connectivity resumes.

4. Multi-Factor Duplicate Prevention Logic

1. Category & Jurisdiction (60%) Strictly matches exact hazard type (e.g. Street Light) and geographic Ward boundary.
2. Spatial Proximity (25%) Calculates distance using Haversine formula between reported GPS markers. Threshold ≤ 150m grants full boost.
3. Token Street Matching (25%) Strips out common stop-words and isolates street names, avenues, and landmarks.
4. Policy Outcome If composite score ≥ 55%, the ticket is flagged as duplicate. Creation of a new work order is blocked; citizen evidence is merged.
